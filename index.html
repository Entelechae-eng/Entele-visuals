5. Frequently Asked Questions (Developer & Creator FAQ)

Are Entelechae Visuals components free for commercial use?

Yes. All 897 React TypeScript and Tailwind CSS motion components are 100% free under the MIT License. You can copy, modify, distribute, and use them in commercial client work, SaaS products, and personal projects without attribution fees.

How do I use Framer and Webflow templates from Entele-Visuals?

Every template card includes a live interactive iframe preview and a direct link to the template's official marketplace listing. For free templates, you can duplicate or remix them directly into your Framer or Webflow account with one click.

Which AI models work best with the Immersive Prompts?

The 683 immersive prompts are optimized for Claude 3.7 Sonnet, Cursor Composer, v0 by Vercel, and OpenAI GPT-4o. Each prompt contains structured tech stacks, animation rules, responsive guidelines, and sensory tone definitions to produce production-ready code.

How does Entelechae Visuals test responsive device compatibility?

Both the template preview modal and the immersive prompt modal feature live multi-viewport frame switches. You can inspect any design in Desktop (16:9), Tablet (4:3), and Mobile (9:16) aspect ratios with active touch scrolling simulation.
